The fastest way to reach us is email: **[CONTACT_EMAIL]**

We read everything and usually reply within a couple of working days.

## What to include

**Reporting a bug in a tool?** The tool name, what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. A sample of the input helps enormously — but please strip anything confidential first, since an email is not the private channel the tool itself is.

**Requesting a new tool?** Tell us what you are trying to do rather than just naming the tool. The underlying problem often has a better solution than the one people ask for.

**Asking about privacy?** See the [privacy policy](/privacy) first — it covers most questions, including how to make a data request. Anything it does not answer, ask.

**Licensing or commercial use?** Get in touch. The tools are free to use, including at work.

## What we cannot help with

We cannot debug your code, your site or your configuration, and we cannot recover a password or a file you did not save. Because the tools run entirely in your browser and store nothing, we have no copy of anything you processed — there is no server-side record to retrieve.

## Reporting a security issue

If you have found a security problem with this site, please email **[CONTACT_EMAIL]** with the details rather than disclosing it publicly, and give us a reasonable window to fix it. We will credit you if you would like.
