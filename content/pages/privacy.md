This policy explains what [SITE_NAME] (“we”, “us”) collects when you use [SITE_DOMAIN], and what we do with it.

## The short version

**The tools on this site do not send your data anywhere.** Every formatter, converter, generator and analyser runs entirely inside your browser using JavaScript. Text you paste and files you upload are processed on your own device and never transmitted to us. We could not read them if we wanted to — there is no server receiving them.

You can verify this yourself: open your browser's developer tools, switch to the Network tab, and use any tool on the site. You will see no outbound request carrying your content.

## What we do not collect

- The text, code, JSON, or documents you paste into any tool
- Images or files you drop into the image and hash tools
- Passwords, API keys, tokens or secrets generated or decoded here
- Any account information, because there are no accounts

Nothing above is stored, logged, or transmitted, because none of it ever leaves your device.

## What is collected automatically

**Server logs.** Like nearly every website, our hosting provider records standard request information — IP address, browser user agent, referring page, and the URL requested. This is used to keep the site running and to detect abuse. These logs are retained for a limited period and are not used to build a profile of you.

**Local storage.** Some tools remember your preferences — an indent width, a selected format — using your browser's local storage. This never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

We do not set tracking cookies of our own.

You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time through [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com), and review how Google handles data at [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

If you decline, the site works exactly the same — every tool is fully functional without consent.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete personal data we hold about you, and to object to its processing.

In practice we hold very little: server logs, and nothing tied to an identity. If you would like to make a request, contact us at [CONTACT_EMAIL] and we will respond within 30 days.

If you are in the EU or UK and are unhappy with our response, you may complain to your national data protection authority.

## Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone else. There is very little to share.
